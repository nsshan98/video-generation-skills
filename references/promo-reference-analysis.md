# Reference Analysis: Modern SaaS & E-commerce Commercial (`07.mp4`)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Format**: 9:16 Vertical Video (1080x1920 @ 30 FPS).
- **Duration**: ~94 seconds.
- **Tone**: Professional, punchy, problem-solution commercial ad for eCommerce merchants.
- **Branding**: Persistent brand anchor (`eCommerceMaker`) with high contrast emerald and light mint themes.

---

## 2. Visual Architecture Breakdown

### A. Background & Texture
- Rather than solid flat color, all scenes use a subtle mathematical dot-grid texture (`patternUnits="userSpaceOnUse"`, spacing: 44px, dot radius: 2.4px).
- The hook scene incorporates an oversized, ultra-faint question mark (`?`) in the center background.

### B. Minimalist Vector Avatars
- Minimalist iconic representation:
  - Head: Circle with two dot eyes and either a clean smile arc or a straight neutral line.
  - Torso: Rounded trapezoid.
  - No realistic limbs, hair, or complex clothing—pure corporate iconography.

### C. Smartphone Device Mockup
- Rounded smartphone bezel (`border: 12px solid #072B20`, `borderRadius: 48px`).
- Features a mock message bubble and an interactive send button with paper airplane icon.
- Accompanied by four floating feature badges (Chat, Receipt, Discount Tag, Star Rating).

### D. Bottom Caption Bar
- Dark emerald rounded capsule positioned at `bottom: 470px`.
- High contrast white text with specific focal words emphasized in `#3BF06D` (Neon Green).
- Typography: Clean Bengali font (Hind Siliguri).
