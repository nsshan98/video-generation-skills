# Reference analysis worksheet

Use only material that can actually be viewed or heard. Record timestamps and distinguish observation from interpretation.

| Time range | Observable detail | Possible purpose | Adaptation for this edit |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | Shot size, movement, cut, text, sound | Hook, emphasis, transition, etc. | What to borrow in principle |

Review:
- Frame shape, composition, subject placement, and camera movement
- Average shot duration and how rhythm changes
- Cut types, transitions, and visual continuity
- Typography, title/lower-third style, placement, and reading time
- Palette, contrast, exposure, and lighting consistency
- Dialogue, music, ambience, and sound effects balance
- Repeated motifs and the opening/closing structure

Do not infer exact lens, LUT, software, or production technique unless directly evidenced. Do not reproduce protected footage or distinctive creative expression shot-for-shot. Note unavailable portions and confidence.
